Q1. A hospital uses a system that asks a series of questions about a
    patient's symptoms and suggests possible diagnoses. What type of
    system is this best described as?

    A) Database management system
    B) Booking system
    C) Expert system
    D) Satellite system

    Correct Answer: C

---

       administrative tasks digitally
