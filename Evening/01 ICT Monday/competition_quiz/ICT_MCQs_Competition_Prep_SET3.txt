Q1. A bank's system automatically flags a transaction as
    suspicious when it detects spending patterns that differ
    significantly from a customer's history. This is an example
    of which type of computer-based system?

    A) Booking system
    B) Satellite system
    C) Expert system applying rule-based reasoning
    D) School management system

    Correct Answer: C

---

Q2. Which of the following statements about ICT in medicine is
    INCORRECT?

    A) Electronic health records allow patient data to be
       shared quickly between departments
    B) ICT eliminates all human error in medical diagnosis
       and treatment completely
    C) Medical imaging software assists radiologists in
       analysing scans
    D) Expert systems can suggest diagnoses based on
       entered symptoms

    Correct Answer: B

---
